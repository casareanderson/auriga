Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Summary

Auriga is a local tool. It does not operate a server, does not create an account for you, and does not send your data anywhere. The extension, bridge and CLI all run on your own computer, and the information they handle stays there.

What Auriga does and does not collect

We do not collect anything

Auriga has no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no cloud backend. We — the developer — do not receive any of your browsing data, logins, cookies, commands or screenshots. There is no account and no server to send anything to.

What stays on your machine

Auriga processes the following locally, and only locally:

  • Tab titles, URLs and page contents, when you ask it to read or act on a page.
  • Cookies, when you use the cookie actions.
  • Screenshots, when you request one.
  • Typed text and executed JavaScript, when you instruct an action.

These exist only for the moment they are needed to carry out the action you requested, and are not stored unless you enable the optional local audit log (see below).

The local audit log

If you enable it, Auriga can append each command to a local JSONL file (~/.auriga/audit.jsonl by default). This file is written to your own disk and is never transmitted. As a safeguard, typed text, evaluated JavaScript and cookie values are redacted before they are written, so secrets do not end up in the log.

You may also choose to ship audit events to your own Ephor instance. That is an optional, self-hosted service you configure and run yourself; Auriga does not ship events to any first-party server.

Permissions

The Chrome extension requests browser permissions (tabs, scripting, cookies, downloads, debugger, and so on) so it can carry out the actions you ask for on sites you approve. These permissions are used to control your browser locally and are not used to collect or transmit information. Per-site approval means nothing runs on a site until you allow it.

Third parties

Auriga does not share information with third parties because it does not collect it. The only network requests it makes are the ones you direct it to make, to the websites you are using.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date. Because Auriga does not operate a service or an account, there is no further notification channel.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised as an issue in the public repository.
